## How to maintain this report

- Before starting each feature, operation, or coherent work chunk, update **Active work** with its status, scope, intended files, and verification plan. Add a dated start entry to the work log.
- At completion, record the actual files changed, observable outcome, tests and results, remaining limitations, and next concrete step. Update the current handoff, clear the active entry, and add a finish entry.
- When a chunk fails, is interrupted, or needs input, record the exact stopping point, error, attempted recovery, pending processes, and next recovery action. Use `failed`, `interrupted`, or `blocked` as appropriate.
- Keep meaningful history. Separate verified results from plans and unresolved acceptance checks. Report test skips and failures explicitly.
- Treat the report update as part of its associated chunk. Its own edits do not require recursively creating another work-log entry.
- Keep recordings, transcript content, secrets, personal identifiers, and machine-specific absolute paths out of this report. Use repository-relative paths.
